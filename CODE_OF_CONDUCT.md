# Code of conduct

GeulPU has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its
code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project — in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project
space — and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good faith, welcome people of every background and level of experience, give and accept
constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks, sexualized language, and publishing someone's private information without their permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, tell the maintainer, [@wwoosshh](https://github.com/wwoosshh). For a private
report, use [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/wwoosshh/GeulPUv2/security/advisories/new) and start the title with
"Conduct:". Reports are handled confidentially. A comment can also be reported to GitHub with **Report content** in the comment's
menu.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of
conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful to the community, following the enforcement guidelines
of the Contributor Covenant.
